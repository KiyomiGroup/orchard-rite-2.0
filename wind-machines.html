Wind Machines & Frost Fans

When one cold night can ruin an entire year’s work, growers rely on Orchard-Rite wind machines — engineered with precision technology since 1967.

The science

Working with the physics of a cold night.

A wind machine doesn’t manufacture heat — it moves warm air that’s already there, pulling it down from the inversion layer into the crop zone below.

Orchard-Rite wind machines rotate 360° to draw warmer air from the inversion layer and mix it into the crop zone below — most effective when that layer sits 15 to 75 feet up.

Winter · Advective freeze

Cold air moves in

Wind pushes a mass of cold air into a region, displacing the warmer air that was there. Most common in the coldest winter months.

Spring & fall · Radiational frost

Heat radiates away

Daytime heat radiates back out of the ground on a clear, calm night. Temperatures near the surface drop fast and frost forms — the exact gap a wind machine closes.
