The two jobs of a harvest

Harvest by day. Defend by night.

Every Orchard-Rite machine answers one of two questions a grower asks every season: how do I get the crop off the tree efficiently, and how do I keep it alive until then. One line does the first job in daylight. The other stands watch after dark.
