Remote monitoring

ORCell™

Monitor and control your frost fans and wind machines from anywhere in the world — on portable units just as reliably as stationary ones.

Know the status of every machine, from anywhere.

ORCell connects your wind machines to a cellular network, then to the internet, giving you live status updates so a frost-risk night doesn’t mean standing in the field to check that everything is still running.

  • Live status for every connected machine, on portable or stationary units
  • Email and text alerts while you’re on the go
  • Adjust settings across your whole fleet remotely
  • Pair with Auto-Start™ for start/stop temperatures accurate to ±0.5°F

ORCell is currently available in select markets. Contact your nearest authorized dealer for availability.
