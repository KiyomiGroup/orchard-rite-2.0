Innovations, since 1990

Built for the longest harvest day.

Each system below solves one specific problem an operator raised in the field — from uneven shaking, to bark damage, to reach into a tall canopy.

AccuShake™

A consistent shake, every tree

Toggle between preset profiles that vary shake time and engine RPM automatically — the uniform removal a skilled operator once had to hold by feel.

WetHead®

Less friction, safer bark

A water-and-silicone injection between the long and short slings lets the rubber glide over the trunk, cutting heat, friction, and the risk of barking.

HydraShake™

Shake tuned to the tree

Precise control over shake aggressiveness and acceleration, tailored to the size and variety of tree instead of running flat-out at engine max.

Side Shooter®

Reach deeper into canopy

An interchangeable tilt attachment for the Monoboom that adds down-the-row reach, then swaps back to standard front-facing shaking when needed.
