A little history

About Orchard-Rite®

From a small warehouse with seven employees to a global provider of frost protection and harvest equipment — without moving the headquarters out of the orchard country that started it.

Manufacturing, Yakima WA

Every part inspected before it leaves the building.

Orchard-Rite’s headquarters sit in the middle of agricultural country, surrounded by the same farms, orchards, and vineyards our machines protect. From the wiring in an Auto-Start box to the paint on a gearbox, nothing ships without a careful check — because the difference between a good season and a great one usually comes down to the details.

Orchard-Rite employees, dealers, and service providers work around the clock so growers have the best experience possible — practical, economical harvest equipment by day, and the industry’s most reliable frost protection by night.

A history of performance
1967

Oil-fired orchard heaters

Co-founder Daryl Hill, raised in rural Washington, sets out to protect neighboring farmers’ crops from frost — starting with oil-fired heaters before rising fuel prices make them impractical.

1972

The first wind machines

After years of hand-cutting prototype blades in his shop, Hill brings Orchard-Rite’s first wind machines to market as a more effective answer to frost.

1990

The first tree shaker

Orchard-Rite answers a second need in the industry: a practical, dependable way to remove nuts from trees at scale.

Today

Two plants, one mission

Two manufacturing and assembly plants in Yakima now supply an estimated 70% of the world’s wind machines, alongside a full tree shaker line.
